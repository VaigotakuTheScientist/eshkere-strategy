# Eshkere Strategy project context

Last updated: 2026-08-15

## Purpose of this document

This is the durable strategic context for future Codex sessions. Keep it concise, update it when meaningful context or decisions emerge, and preserve ambiguity where the strategy or broader Eshkere concept is still evolving.

## Confirmed context

- This repository is **Eshkere Strategy**: the founder's personal strategic system and workspace.
- Its immediate purpose is to help understand the founder's goals, map resources and power, identify bottlenecks, plan actions, and track projects.
- Eshkere Strategy is a personal strategy/project for increasing the founder's power and resources, especially to contribute more effectively to AI Safety.
- **Eshkere** is a broader, evolving philosophy/name. It currently consists mainly of that philosophy and name plus several projects and plans in development; it is not yet a formal organization.
- Its current core idea can be summarized as: **“Eshkere is a simple company. We maximize Health and play Games.”** This is a rough formulation, not a complete or final definition.
- “Eshkere is a simple company” is currently a framing or joke, not a settled claim about legal or organizational structure.
- **Health** means the non-optional underlying needs and conditions for human flourishing: physical, mental, and social well-being. It is the serious, non-optional part of the framework.
- **Games** means the optional ways humans pursue enjoyment, achievement, identity, exploration, status, creativity, meaning, and similar ends. Games are what people choose to do once the underlying constraints of being human are taken seriously.
- Eshkere Strategy and a future Eshkere organization or company must not be assumed to be the same thing.
- AI Safety is currently extremely important to the founder, but Eshkere must not be reduced to or automatically characterized as an AI Safety organization.
- A website is not currently an established goal. It may be considered later, but no public website or website implementation should be assumed.

## Repository role

Early context-establishment stage. The repository is intended to become a persistent private system for strategic understanding, planning, and project tracking. Its practical structure and operating cadence are not yet decided.

## Working hypotheses

- Eshkere could combine public-good work that improves Health with projects undertaken because they are worthwhile Games.
- AI Safety may become a major part of Eshkere's work.
- Eshkere may eventually become a formal organization, but this is not decided.
- Eshkere Strategy may eventually use structured goal, resource, bottleneck, action, and project records; the useful format is not yet decided.
- If a website is later wanted, it could become an organization homepage, manifesto/worldview, project hub, or some combination.
