## Confirmed context

- **AI Safety is the ultimate objective.** Power and resources are instrumental to contributing more effectively, not terminal goals.
- Over roughly the next year, the main objective is to remove the current financial bottleneck while continuing to build an AI-safety career.
- Removing that bottleneck means reliably covering ordinary life, preserving monthly room for career travel/equipment/workspace, building runway, and retaining relocation option value.

- Income must not be maximized in isolation from AI-safety value, health, time, and long-run career capital.

### Broader Eshkere

- **Eshkere** is a broader, evolving philosophy/name plus several projects and plans. It is not yet a formal organization.
- Its rough framing is: **“Eshkere is a simple company. We maximize Health and play Games.”**
- “Company” is currently framing/a joke, not a settled legal or organizational form.
- **Health** means the serious, non-optional conditions for human flourishing: physical, mental, and social well-being.
- **Games** means the optional pursuits through which people seek enjoyment, achievement, identity, exploration, status, creativity, meaning, and related ends.
- Eshkere Strategy and a future Eshkere organization/company are not assumed to be the same.
- AI Safety may become a major part of Eshkere, but Eshkere is not assumed to be simply an AI-safety organization.

## Strategic model

Use:

**Current state → bottlenecks → strategies → opportunities → prioritization → next actions**

### Power categories

1. **Capital** — money, savings/runway, assets, equipment, compute, housing/workspace, travel, and relocation capacity.
2. **Capabilities** — knowledge, skills, experience, credentials, judgment, execution ability, and useful information.
3. **Productivity** — usable time, energy, attention, health insofar as it affects action, tools, systems, and working conditions.
4. **Influence** — relationships, network, reputation, audience, trust, organizational position, authority, and coordination capacity.

Compute, audience, and reputation are normally subcomponents, not additional top-level forms of power.

### Default constraints

Strategies should be legal, ethically defensible, compatible with protecting health, and not counterproductive to AI Safety. Maximizing power never means maximizing it regardless of cost. More specific principles and tradeoffs remain open.

### Resource-acquisition routes

The routes can be combined:

1. **Employment** — especially realistic AI-safety-aligned generalist, operations, community, communications, and project roles; technical or advanced roles remain in scope when credible. Includes contracting/consulting.
2. **Career Transition Grants** — time-bounded personal runway for moving into higher-impact work.
3. **Project Funding** — grants or sponsorship for AI-safety projects.
4. **Fellowships / Internships / Residencies / Support Programs** — income, in-kind support, credentials, output, or network while capabilities are developed.
5. **Project Revenue & Donations** — resources from worthwhile projects, services/products, media, events, prizes, sponsorship, donations, or entrepreneurship.

These are acquisition mechanisms, not career paths. Career paths may include generalist work, field-building, technical AI safety, governance/policy, entrepreneurship, and others.

## Decision rules under uncertainty

- Do not stop because a fact was not supplied by the user when a reversible choice can be made.
- Preserve unknown personal facts as `TBD`; use clearly labeled estimates, hypotheses, ranges, and conservative defaults to make progress.
- Prefer choices that create information, evidence, or option value at bounded cost.
- Ask the user only when a decision requires new authority, creates an irreversible/material external consequence, or presents risk that cannot be bounded by a reversible default.
- Do not invent experience, eligibility, application answers, project history, or financial facts.

## Information handling

- Treat all tracked content as public.
- The user explicitly permits useful personal operational data, including approximate financial ranges and opportunity/application details.
- Avoid unnecessary precision. Use the gitignored local `private/` directory when privacy is requested or publication creates material risk without strategic value.
- Never commit credentials, banking/account data, identity documents or numbers, precise addresses, private medical records, or confidential information from other people.
