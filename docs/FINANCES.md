## 2026 benchmark anchors

- Sweden's 2026 riksnorm is **SEK 5,220/month for one adult**. It covers food, clothing, leisure, consumables, health/hygiene, newspaper, and phone, but excludes housing, electricity, work travel, and home insurance ([Government of Sweden](https://www.regeringen.se/artiklar/2025/11/riksnorm-for-2026-faststalld/)).
- Konsumentverket's one-person benchmark is roughly **SEK 8,400–8,570/month before rent, transport, and most medical costs**. It is a modeled household budget, not observed spending, and assumes a relatively well-equipped one-person household ([2026 calculations](https://stpubshop.blob.core.windows.net/publikationer/kostnadsberakningar-hushallskostnader-2026-sv-kov_tga_32d.pdf), [method](https://www.konsumentverket.se/ekonomi/vilka-kostnader-har-ett-hushall/)).
- Current MKB student listings provide a lower Malmö housing anchor; student rent is normally paid for ten months and includes electricity, heat, and water ([listings](https://www.mkbfastighet.se/lediga-lagenheter?sida=1), [student terms](https://www.mkbfastighet.se/student/)). Ordinary small-flat listings provide the future-robustness scenario.
- A Malmö 30-day ticket is SEK 655 before discounts; a qualifying student's stable-planning price is about SEK 491. A temporary 2026 half-price subsidy is not assumed to persist ([Skånetrafiken](https://www.skanetrafiken.se/biljetter/priser-och-rabatter/)).
- A conservative health sinking fund of about SEK 438/month annualizes the public outpatient-care and covered-prescription ceilings. It does not cover all dental or non-covered care ([1177 Skåne](https://www.1177.se/Skane/sa-fungerar-varden/kostnader-och-ersattningar/hogkostnadsskydd-for-oppenvard/), [TLV](https://www.tlv.se/lakemedelsforetag/hogkostnadsskyddet.html)).

Definitions:

- **Essential burn** is the cost of maintaining a viable life without routine discretionary or career-investment spending.
- **Normal burn** adds ordinary household consumption and a deliberate career/equipment/travel sinking fund.
- Student rent is annualized for burn, but paid-month cash needs can be roughly SEK 0.7–1.2k higher.
- The career reserves of SEK 0.5k, 1.5k, and 3k are planning choices, not sourced observations.

## Calculation method

- `essential burn = essential recurring costs`
- `normal burn = essential burn + normal recurring additions`
- `monthly net burn = chosen burn basis − dependable monthly support`
- `usable liquidity = spendable savings − committed near-term obligations`
- `current runway = usable liquidity ÷ positive monthly net burn`
